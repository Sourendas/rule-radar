RuleRadar — app map

One action traced through the code: press “Scan rules”.

1. Input — index.html #rules textarea and #scan button. Listener at the bottom of src/app.js.
2. Orchestrate — src/app.js run() saves the text, reads settings (home timezone, country) and calls scanRules().
3. Extract — src/scanner.js findDates() (regex DATE_RE), pickDeadline(), checkEligibility(), findRequirements() (table REQS).
4. Convert — src/tz.js resolveZone() → toUtc() (two-pass IANA offset, handles DST) → formatInZone().
5. Show — src/app.js render() builds the Deadline, Eligibility, Checklist and Key-dates cards; countdown() ticks every second; ticks go to localStorage key rr:ticks:<hash>.

Where to change things: new zone word → tz.js FIXED/REGION; new checklist rule → scanner.js REQS; look → styles.css :root. Tests: test/scanner.test.js.
